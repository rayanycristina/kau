# KAU Product Architecture

## North Star
KAU is the operating system for commercial performance. For the AlphaSin operation, the first operational KPI is daily revenue. The seller-level KPI is commission generated today. Initial commission rule: 5% of revenue closed by each seller.

## Modules
1. Command Center: the nervous system of the operation.
2. Live Call Copilot: realtime transcript, objection detection, emotional analysis, next-best-action.
3. Call Review: post-call score, timeline replay, seller evolution plan.
4. Conversational Pipeline: adaptive lead context and opportunity heat.
5. Follow-up Machine: pressure queue for execution.
6. Money Alert Center: revenue leak escalation and forced action.
7. COD/PAD Intelligence: delivery/payment/refusal prediction.
8. Sales Arena: elite gamification and prestige progression.
9. Coach AI: seller skill evolution.
10. Revenue Leak Detector: risk attribution and projected losses.
11. CEO Briefing: executive control and strategic forecast.

## Operational Rules
- Any opportunity with high heat and no action becomes money at risk.
- Overdue follow-ups escalate visual intensity over time.
- Seller inactivity is a team-level risk event.
- AI output must always suggest an action: call, message, reschedule, confirm, escalate.
