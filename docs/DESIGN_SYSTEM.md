# KAU Design System

## Visual Atmosphere
Obsidian dark OS, metallic graphite surfaces, petroleum blue depth, neon semantic accents.

## Semantic Color Logic
- Green: revenue, opportunity, completed execution.
- Amber: pending, scheduled, requires attention.
- Red: revenue leak, risk, overdue, critical.
- Cyan: intelligence, AI, data, analysis.
- Purple: gamification, XP, prestige, progression.

## Component Principles
Components are not cards. They are tactical instruments. Every component needs a job, a pulse, and an action.

## Motion
- Counter updates: fast numeric lift with subtle glow.
- Critical escalation: red pulse at 1.6s rhythm.
- AI thinking: cyan waveform and breathing halo.
- Mission completion: restrained glow burst and short sound.
- Panel transition: 0.72s cinematic reveal with blur release.
